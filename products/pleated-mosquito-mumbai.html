Maintenance Guide for Long-Term Smooth Operation

Pleated nets are low-maintenance, but periodic care keeps them operating smoothly. Monthly dry dust cleaning and occasional track wiping are usually enough for regular homes. Avoid pulling the mesh aggressively and avoid force-closing to preserve pleat shape.

In high-dust locations or near traffic corridors, light cleaning every two to three weeks can significantly improve life and movement quality.

  1. Brush or vacuum dust from mesh folds
  2. Wipe tracks with soft dry cloth
  3. Check for any debris in bottom guide
  4. Call service team for alignment if movement becomes hard
